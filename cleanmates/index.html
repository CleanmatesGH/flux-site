🧹 CleanMates

Chore rotas, shared supplies and household spending for house shares.

CleanMates gives a house share one shared place for the admin: who is doing which chore, what the house has run out of, and who has paid for what. It is built for UK renters and students, and it is free to use.

What it does

  • Chore rotas — set up recurring chores, assign them to one or more housemates, and rotate them automatically so the same person is not always on bins.
  • Points and a leaderboard — bigger jobs are worth more, so effort is visible rather than argued about.
  • Shared supplies — a running list of what the house needs, and who bought it last.
  • Household spending — record what things cost, see who is up and who is down, and settle up. CleanMates never touches your money; it only keeps the record.
  • Message board — house notices in one place instead of buried in a group chat.
  • Deals — occasional offers from retailers that are relevant to a shared house. These are affiliate links, and we say so in the app.

Get the app

CleanMates is available on the App Store for iPhone. An Android version is in preparation.
